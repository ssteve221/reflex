## Timing tips

- **Over by 1–2 minutes**: Cut the spreadsheet import demo from Run 1 if needed — the JSON paste demo is faster and equally compelling.
- **Over by 3+ minutes**: The Architecture slide is the most likely to over-run. Prepare a 30-second version and a 90-second version.
- **Under by 2+ minutes**: Slow down the live demo. Narrate what you're clicking and why — panels respect calm, deliberate demos over rushed ones.
